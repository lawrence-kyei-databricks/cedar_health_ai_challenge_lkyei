# Business Question

Which critical-risk patients have the highest financial exposure with multiple open care gaps,
and what is their total readmission cost risk to Cedar Health?

## Context
Cedar Health loses ~$45M annually to avoidable 30-day readmissions (~3,000/yr at $15K each).
Care coordinators need to prioritize outreach to patients with the highest combined risk:
- Critical risk band (readmission_risk_score >= 0.9)
- Multiple open care gaps (open_gap_count >= 2)
- High financial exposure per patient

This query identifies the top 10 patients requiring immediate coordinator intervention,
sorted by financial exposure. These are the patients where a single prevented readmission
saves Cedar Health $14,250.
